About RetinaX Care

RetinaX Care

Closing the Loop in Diabetic Eye Care

RetinaX Care is a diabetic eye-screening coordination and follow-up platform designed to keep diabetes-care teams connected with patients across every step: screening identification → scheduling → retinal scans → specialist review → outreach → care loop completion.

Safety & Scope Notice:
RetinaX Care is strictly an assistive care coordination platform. All clinical assessments and medical determinations remain the sole responsibility of qualified healthcare professionals.
